**Name:** Memova

**Subtitle:** Find meeting notes, read Sparks, and manage selected knowledge.

**Category:** Productivity

**Description:**

Memova helps you find and read your meeting notes, Spark conversations, and generated Markdown or
HTML files. Retrieve a complete Spark discussion, including its original operation commands, or
choose a specific generated file to read or download.

Preview selected text before saving it to Memova, review Knowledge Entry proposals before applying
them, and inspect or run existing automation tasks with their required approvals. In supported
Codex environments, you can also create a Personal Manual from up to 20 authorized evidence items
and archive selected final outputs. Personal Manual generation uploads derived results rather than
raw conversations and publishes the result at an unlisted link accessible to anyone who has it.

The Plugin does not collect your complete conversation history in the background. Agent-output
archiving is a Codex/Mac beta and defaults to explicit requests. Local setup and connection helpers
require a compatible local environment.

**Hold before upload:** This text omits general Knowledge V5 retrieval until its availability is
resolved. Current manifest still advertises it. Host-specific claims must be verified in Portal and
real host acceptance. Knowledge Entry and archive operations also need reviewer-account acceptance.

## Starter prompts

1. Find my latest Meeting or Spark file and let me choose what to read.
2. Find the Spark called “Atlas Launch Discussion” and show its complete conversation, including commands.
3. Show my unfinished Memova automation tasks and explain which still need my confirmation. Do not run them.

Additional Personal Manual prompt for supported Codex acceptance:
“Generate and publish my Personal Manual using only the three excerpts I provide here. I accept
Memova's disclosed privacy practices. Do not read any other tasks or conversations.”

The first three are a proposal, not confirmed Portal prompt slots or a manifest edit.
